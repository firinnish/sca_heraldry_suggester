Charge groups

Every ordinary with charges around it, above it, below it and on it; charges with other charges around and on them; and everything inside a chief, base, bordure or orle. Look for: charges that touch or overlap something, charges far too small, and charges not where a herald would expect them. A caption in red means the tools have no arrangement for it (nothing is drawn), which is right wherever the Glossary's Table 7 gives no default. The placing is in core/layout.js.
