Traceable Heraldic Art pictures: drawing check

Every picture in charges/tha/ (listed in charges/tha.csv), three of it in a colour on a metal. Look for: a charge the colour does not fill (the background shows through its inside), a charge that is only an outline, lines that have gone missing, and anything that is not a charge to place on a shield. They are fetched a few at a time, so the page fills in as you watch.
